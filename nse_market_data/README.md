# NSE Market Data Analytics and Engineering

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on building a market data ingestion,
validation, transformation and analytics solution using
historical equity market data from the National Stock Exchange
of India (NSE).

The project will progressively evolve from a Python-based CSV
ingestion exercise into a structured data engineering and
financial analytics pipeline.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Ingest historical NSE equity market data.
- Validate source data quality and consistency.
- Identify unusual price and volume movements.
- Calculate financial and market indicators.
- Store historical market data systematically.
- Build a reusable and extensible data pipeline.
- Enable analytical reporting.
